Research

The Effect of Risk-Based Capital (RBC) and Underwriting Ratio on Return on Assets (ROA) at PT Asuransi Bhakti Bhayangkara (2020–2024)

Undergraduate thesis | UIN Sunan Gunung Djati Bandung | May 2026 | Grade A

  • Analyzed 60 monthly financial observations over 5 years, using 3 insurance financial indicators.
  • Applied multiple linear regression and statistical tests in SPSS and Gretl.
  • Result: RBC and Underwriting Ratio each have a positive and significant effect on ROA. The model explains 57.2% of ROA variation.
  • Strengthened the findings with interviews of validators and a qualitative analysis from an Islamic business ethics perspective.

A Comprehensive Analysis of Gender Disparities in Indonesian Human Development Using Machine Learning Techniques

Second author | The 1st International Conference on Islamic Economics (ICIE), UIN K.H. Abdurrahman Wahid Pekalongan | September 2024

  • Studied gender gaps in life expectancy, education, and spending across Indonesian regions, using 2023 Human Development Index data.
  • Used A/B testing, composite scoring, and K-Means clustering. The charts are shown at the top of this page.
  • Main finding: 78.6% of regions rank “High” for men, against 13.4% for women, with the widest gaps in remote areas such as Papua.
